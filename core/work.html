📈 Investment Data

I have experience developing ETL scripts for investment data for hundreds of clients across 30+ custodians and have worked with a variety of standards and methodologies related to data, bookkeeping, and accounting. The quality of source data ranged from dirty (common) to pristine (rarely) which provided a ton of experience investigating issues, handling errors, and applying best practices.

Having previously held FINRA Series 7 and 66 financial licenses, I am comfortable working with all sorts of transaction types and corporate actions, and can discern between acquisitions and unsigned dispositions. Many clients did not use negative numbers for dispositions, so corporate actions were often a challenge for data analysts without subject knowledge. But I was able to support the team in understanding the data and properly categorizing transactions, as well as communicating with clients/custodians about the types of transactions and securities we needed to collect.

I also created scripts to reconcile accounts in order to help detect gaps in data in case funds had transferred, merged, closed, errored, or encountered unexpected behavior.
